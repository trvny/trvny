# TRAVNY

> Public hub for TVP IPTV, local weather, enhanced feeds, browser workbenches and small technical publications.

Canonical site: https://bartek.lol/. The hub also opens directly at https://travny.pages.dev/.

Creator: Bartek (TRAVNY / trvny); the `travnie` GitHub organization hosts companion projects. [GitHub profile](https://github.com/trvny) · [Organization](https://github.com/travnie).

## Services

- [TRAVNY Radio](https://bartek.lol/radio/): live experimental MP3 station, 192 kb/s; [listen on hub](https://bartek.lol/#radio), [stream](https://radio.bartek.lol/radio.mp3), [now playing JSON](https://bartek.lol/api/radio-station?station=travny), [machine guide](https://bartek.lol/radio/index.md). The station is Internet-only; no terrestrial FM frequency.

- Home radio: TRAVNY Radio is the default live MP3 station with track metadata
  and album art. Swipe or use arrows to switch Radio Paradise, Trójka, Czwórka
  and BBC Radio 1. Live tracks or programmes where available; station logos as
  fallback. TRAVNY metadata: `/api/radio-station?station=travny`; Paradise:
  `/api/radio-now`; other stations: `/api/radio-station?station=bbc1`.
- [Bench Lab](https://bartek.lol/lab/): Streambench, Codebench and Docbench carousel.
- [TVPI](https://bartek.lol/tv/): browser page for stable TVP IPTV entry points and playlists.
- [Weather](https://weather.bartek.lol/): multi-source local weather for Kościelec in Chrzanów.
- [Feedseek](https://rss.bartek.lol/): enhanced Atom/RSS directory, JSON Feed sidecars, OPML and Reader.
- [Streambench](https://streambench.bartek.lol/): IPTV, radio, HLS, M3U/M3U8 and XMLTV workshop.
- [Codebench](https://codebench.bartek.lol/): local-first QR and barcode studio.
- [Docbench](https://docbench.bartek.lol/): local-first document and PDF studio.
- [Badges](https://bartek.lol/badges/): ecosystem, bot, AI and live
  status/counter SVG badges with copyable Markdown and HTML.
- [Teksty](https://bartek.lol/teksty/): articles, experiments and Reactor notes, including GitHubowa Kraina and Tokenowy Mundial AI.
- [CoCzyDziennik](https://bartek.lol/przeglad/): one archive of all dated news digests, newest first.
