# TRAVNY Teksty

> Static guide to TRAVNY publications. The [live Teksty directory](https://travny.pages.dev/teksty/) and [unified Atom feed](https://travny.pages.dev/teksty/feed.xml) cover the current publication stream. Atom includes at most 100 entries.

## Reactor

- [Latest editorial note](https://shitpost-reactor.travny.workers.dev/): the newest Reactor entry is featured dynamically on the human `/teksty/` page from its JSON Feed, without copying it into this publication catalog.

## CoCzyDziennik

- [CoCzyDziennik archive](https://travny.pages.dev/przeglad/): dated Markdown news editions, also listed alongside the articles and editorial notes in the same Teksty directory and Atom feed. Individual issue URLs remain under `/przeglad/YYYY-MM-DD/`.

## Existing articles

- [Alpaka, krab i Wielki Harmonizator (Polski)](https://travny.pages.dev/teksty/grass-mud-horse-pl/): a Polish adaptation of the Chinese internet mythology essay, with original storytelling and slang examples.
- [The Alpaca, the Crab and the Censor (English)](https://travny.pages.dev/teksty/grass-mud-horse/): how Mandarin profanity, fictional internet animals and censorship collide.
- [GitHubowa Kraina](https://travny.pages.dev/teksty/githubowa-kraina/): interactive atlas of the 12 public repositories split between `trvny` and `travnie`, with project regions, the shared tool district and infrastructure.
- [Tokenowy Mundial AI](https://travny.pages.dev/teksty/token-worldcup/): equivalent natural text in 27 languages with OpenAI and Claude tokenization comparisons.
