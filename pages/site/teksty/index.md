# TRAVNY Teksty

> Static guide to TRAVNY publications. The [live Teksty directory](https://bartek.lol/teksty/) and [unified Atom feed](https://bartek.lol/teksty/feed.xml) cover the current publication stream. Atom includes at most 100 entries.

## Reactor

- [Latest editorial note](https://shitpost.bartek.lol/): the newest Reactor entry is featured dynamically on the human `/teksty/` page from its JSON Feed, without copying it into this publication catalog.

## CoCzyDziennik

- [CoCzyDziennik archive](https://bartek.lol/przeglad/): dated Markdown news editions, also listed alongside the articles and editorial notes in the same Teksty directory and Atom feed. Individual issue URLs remain under `/przeglad/YYYY-MM-DD/`.

## Existing articles

- [Alpaka, krab i Wielki Harmonizator / The Alpaca, the Crab and the Censor](https://bartek.lol/teksty/grass-mud-horse/): one bilingual article (PL / EN) on Mandarin profanity, mythical internet animals and censorship. Switch language on the page; legacy Polish links redirect to `?lang=pl`.
- [GitHubowa Kraina](https://bartek.lol/teksty/githubowa-kraina/): interactive atlas of `trvny` and `travnie` projects, with explorable regions, the shared tool district and infrastructure.
- [Tokenowy Mundial AI](https://bartek.lol/teksty/token-worldcup/): equivalent natural text in 27 languages with OpenAI and Claude tokenization comparisons.
